# Gemini CLI context

This repository's contract for every AI tool is [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md). Read it, then
[`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md) (the full house rules), [`docs/PROJECT-STATE.md`](docs/PROJECT-STATE.md)
(the current handoff) and the latest [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md) entry before changing anything.

The staff portal is in daily use: do not change behaviour staff rely on unless the owner asked.
`PUSH.bat` is the only release path, and nothing local counts as released until it has run.
The ERP additions (HRM, CRM, inventory, sales, finance) are documented in
[`docs/ERP-ROADMAP.md`](docs/ERP-ROADMAP.md) and held by `tests/business-joins.mjs`.
