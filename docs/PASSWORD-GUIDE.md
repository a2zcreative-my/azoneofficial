# PASSWORD-GUIDE.md — changing your password (v1.4.9)

One rule for everyone: passwords are **10 characters minimum**, and changing a
password signs that account out of **every other device immediately** — which
is exactly what you want if a password may have leaked.

Every password box has an eye icon to show what you typed.

---

## Staff (HR, Sales & Marketing, CCO, COO, CEO, and all portal roles)

1. Sign in at **azoneofficial.com/login** with your company email + password.
2. Open **Profile** (last tab in the portal).
3. Scroll to **Change password**: current password → new password → confirm.
4. You stay signed in on this device; every other device is signed out.

Forgot it? Ask an admin — they set a temporary password from
/admin → Users → **Reset password** on your row, hand it to you directly, and
you change it in Profile after signing in.

## Admin team (admin, editor, marketing, super admin)

Same as staff, but the form lives in **/admin → Account**.

## Customers / clients

1. Sign in at **azoneofficial.com/login**.
2. You land in **/account** — use its **Change password** section.
3. Signed in with Google? There is no password on our system to change —
   your Google account is your key; manage it at myaccount.google.com.

## Admins resetting someone else's password

/admin → Users → **Reset password** on their row → set a temporary password
(10+ chars) → tell them **directly** (WhatsApp or in person — never somewhere
it lingers) → they change it themselves at first sign-in. Setting it revokes
all their sessions. Admins cannot reset a super admin's password.
